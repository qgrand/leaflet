## What

`app/api/src/generate/archetype.rs`'s `FieldDef` declares `field_type`,
`required`, `section` and `keys`, but no `options`. `"select"` is in the
declared field vocabulary and the JS engine's own wizard reads
`field.options`, so the Rust side simply cannot express a select's
choices. The Creator Studio wizard (`BB26091204`) renders a `<select>`
for the type and reads `options` off the schema — against today's engine
that control can only ever show "Select…" and nothing else.

Add `options: Option<&'static [&'static str]>` to `FieldDef` and to
`FIELD_DEFAULTS`, and surface it in the schema endpoint's JSON.

## Notes

**Not fixed in `BB26091204`'s own session, deliberately.** Adding a field
to `FieldDef` breaks every archetype entry that spells its fields out in
full rather than using `..FIELD_DEFAULTS` — there are about ten of those
(`section: Some("Header"), keys: None` and similar), and they would each
need the new field added. That is a mechanical change, but a mechanical
change across ten call sites with **no compiler available** is exactly
the shape of edit that looks right and is not: no Rust builds on this
machine at all (`BH26091501`). It is one small, confident change once
`cargo check` runs again.

**No archetype in `_common` uses `select` today**, so nothing is visibly
broken right now. That is why this is a real gap rather than an urgent
one: the first archetype that wants a dropdown discovers it, and by then
the wizard looks like the thing at fault.
